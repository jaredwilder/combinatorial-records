# Conference switching cannot realize the target Ramsey-book family

**Author:** Jared Wilder  
**Source campaign:** 2026-07-22  
**Public routing:** 2026-09-11

## Construction-class theorem

Let `N=4m+2`, and let `C` be any symmetric conference matrix of order `N`. Consider graphs obtained from the associated Seidel/conference construction after arbitrary diagonal `±1` switching.

Then no such switched conference construction yields a graph that simultaneously

- avoids the book `B_m`, and
- has complement avoiding `B_{m+1}`.

For the campaign instance `N=398=4*99+2`, no switching of any symmetric conference matrix of order 398 can simultaneously avoid `B_99` while its complement avoids `B_100`.

This is a **construction-class impossibility theorem**. It is not a nonexistence theorem for all graphs at the ambient Ramsey-book target.

## Proof character

The recovered source describes an exact row-sum contradiction uniform over the whole symmetric-conference switching class. It does not enumerate individual switchings.

The historical packet records a producer, an independent replay verifier, a one-command verification script and a receipt, but those standalone files were not recovered into the current public sweep. The theorem statement is therefore routed here while exact recovery of those original bytes remains a provenance task.
